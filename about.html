MartLink Solutions exists to give Karachi's local marts, grocery stores, and restaurants the same digital tools the big platforms have — without giving up their profit to get them.

Our Story

Why MartLink Solutions started.

MartLink Solutions was founded on a simple observation: local marts and restaurants across Karachi were paying a huge share of their profit to third-party delivery platforms, just to reach their own customers online.

These businesses had no real alternative — building a proper website or app on their own meant dealing with developers, hosting, security, and constant upkeep, none of which they had the time or technical background for.

MartLink was built to close that gap: a branded online store that belongs entirely to the business, fully built and maintained by us, with no commission and no technical burden on the owner.

I am Ayaz Tabassum, Founder of MartLink Solutions.

My retail journey spans over 12 years at Imtiaz Super Market, where I worked my way up from the ground level — as a GRN Maker, Till Cashier, Checkout Supervisor, and later in Checkout Management & Support Management. That experience gave me a complete, inside-out understanding of how a retail store truly operates and where it loses its growth potential.

While in Support Management, I noticed a common but costly problem: hundreds of quality products were marked as slow-moving, not because they were bad products, but because they lacked visibility on the physical shelf.

I decided to contribute beyond my assigned role. I took the initiative to list those slow-moving items on online channels like Daraz and Facebook Marketplace to increase store sales. The result was immediate — products that were not moving in-store started selling online.

About six months into this successful initiative, our branch got a new Branch Manager. With a very limited and traditional mindset, he found this digital initiative unusual. Instead of evaluating its positive sales impact, he focused only on the perceived risks and reported the activity to upper management as a deviation from procedure.

Following that report, my journey with the company ended and I was asked to resign.

"At that time, people around me saw it as a failure. For me, it was clarity."

The very idea that ended my job in july 2024, is the same idea that is now my vision, my career, and my company.

That vision is MartLink Solutions.
