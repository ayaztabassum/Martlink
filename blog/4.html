DAY 3

Website + Android + iOS App Design

Hum aapke brand colors me premium UI banate hain - Imtiaz jaisa. Homepage pe offers, daily deals, search. Customer ko app download karne ki zaroorat nahi, website se bhi order kar sakta hai.

DAY 4

Pricing, Stock & WhatsApp Alerts

Daily price change ka sabse easy system - ek jagah price badlo, website + apps me auto update. Har new order ka alert aapke WhatsApp pe direct - koi order miss nahi hoga.

Hum 50 test orders karte hain - order, payment, rider, WhatsApp sab check. Phir aapke mart pe aake aapko aur aapke ladkon ko 1 ghante ki training - itna easy ke 10th pass bhi chala le.
