Online Grocery Business Kaise Start Karein? Complete Guide for Mart Owners in 2026

By MartLink PK Team | May 14, 2026 | Karachi, Pakistan | 8 min read
MartLink Solutions by Ayaz - Empowering Local Stores Digitally

Karachi me agar aapka kiryana store, super mart ya grocery shop hai, to ab offline se online jana zarurat ban gaya hai. Customer ab WhatsApp pe price puchne ke bajaye direct app pe order karna chahta hai. Is guide me hum step-by-step batayenge ke online grocery business kaise start karein — woh bhi Foodpanda ki 30% commission ke baghair.

Quick Answer: Online grocery business start karne ke liye aapko chahiye: (1) Apni branded Website + Android + iOS App, (2) Admin Panel jahan 5000+ products add kar saken, (3) Rider App with live tracking, (4) WhatsApp auto-order alerts. MartLink PK ye complete system Rs. 60,000 setup me 7 din me ready karke deta hai — 0% commission.

Step 1: Business Model Decide Karein

Online grocery ke 2 models hain:

  • Marketplace Model (Galat): Foodpanda / Pandamart jaisa — aapka store unki list me, commission 20-35%, customer unka. Aapka brand khatam.
  • Branded Model (Sahi): Apni website apnamart.com aur apna app "Apna Mart - Grocery Delivery". Customer aapka, data aapka, profit 100% aapka. Yehi model MartLink Solutions deta hai.

Step 2: Products Ki List Aur Pricing Ready Karein

Start me 800-1500 fast-moving items se start karein:

  1. Daal, chawal, atta, ghee, oil
  2. Biscuits, chips, cold drinks
  3. Daalain, masalay, soaps

MartLink ke admin panel me aap roz price 2 minute me update kar sakte hain — Excel upload ka option bhi hai.

Step 3: Apna Online System Banwayen - 7 Din Me

Ye sabse important step hai. Aapko developer dhoondne ki zarurat nahi:

1
Day 1-2: Branding & Setup
Hum aapka logo, colors, banner aur 500 products add karte hain. Domain + Hosting included.
2
Day 3-4: Website + Customer Apps
Customer ke liye website + Android + iOS app — same design. Search, cart, online payment (JazzCash / Easypaisa / COD), delivery slots.
3
Day 5: Admin + Manager + Rider App
Admin Panel (full control), Manager Login (aapke partner ke liye limited access), Rider App (order accept → pickup → delivered with live tracking).
4
Day 6-7: WhatsApp & Launch
Har naya order seedha aapke WhatsApp pe. Training + Play Store upload + QR code for your shop.

Step 4: Delivery System Set Karein

Start me 1-2 riders enough hain 3km radius ke liye. MartLink Rider App me:

  • Rider ko order ka notification
  • Customer ko live tracking — “Rider 10 min me aayega”
  • Delivery ke baad COD collect ka hisaab

Step 5: Marketing - 0 Budget Se 3X Order Kaise Layen?

1. Shop Par QR Code Lagayen

“Apna App Download Karein - 10% OFF First Order” — ye sabse taiz tareeka hai.

2. WhatsApp Broadcast

Roz ke 2-3 offers apne purane customers ko bhejein. MartLink me auto WhatsApp status image generator bhi hai.

3. SEO + Google My Business

“Grocery delivery near me in Gulshan” pe aapka store aaye — iske liye MartLink SEO setup karke deta hai jo aapke blogs (jaise ye 7.html) se hota hai.

Kitna Kharcha Aur Kitna Munafa?

  • Setup: Rs. 60,000 (Website + Android + iOS + Admin + Rider App + WhatsApp)
  • Monthly: Rs. 40,000 (Server, maintenance, Play Store updates, support)
  • Commission: 0% — 1000 Rs ke order pe 1000 Rs aapka
  • Foodpanda se comparison: 1000 Rs pe 700 Rs milta, 300 Rs commission. 100 orders/day = Rs. 30,000/day ka nuqsan.

Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Naye Log Karte Hain

  1. Freelancer se sasta app banwana — 2 mahine baad band.
  2. Poore Karachi ko target karna — pehle 3km pe focus karein.
  3. Manual WhatsApp par orders lena — scale nahi hota. System chahiye.

Final Word: 2026 me online grocery business start karna mushkil nahi, agar system sahi ho. Aapko coding seekhne ki zarurat nahi — MartLink Solutions aapka technical partner hai. Hum Karachi ke local stores ko hi serve karte hain, isliye aapka dard samajhte hain.
