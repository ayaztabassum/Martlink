Benefits of Digitalizing Your Mart / Super Market in Karachi in 2026 (With Comparison)

By MartLink PK Team | May 13, 2026 | Karachi, Pakistan

In 2026, every customer in Karachi — from DHA to Gulshan — first checks their phone before visiting a mart. If your super market is still only offline, you are losing 60% of daily orders to Imtiaz, Carrefour, and your neighbour mart who has his own app.

Digitalizing your mart does not mean listing on Foodpanda and paying 30% commission. It means having your own branded website + Android & iOS apps + Rider App like big brands. Here are real benefits we have seen with 100+ marts in Karachi.

7 Major Benefits of Digitalizing Your Super Market

1. 📈 3x More Orders

Physical store = customers within 500m. Digital mart = entire area (Gulshan, Bahria, DHA). Average mart sees 2.5x to 3x order growth in 90 days.

2. 💰 0% Commission, 100% Profit

Foodpanda takes Rs.300 on Rs.1000. Your own app by MartLink = Rs.1000 is fully yours. Customer data (number, address) also 100% yours.

3. 🕒 Open 24/7, Even When Shutter Closed

Customers can order at 11pm for next morning delivery. No rent for extra hours, but sales continue.

4. 📊 Secured Manager Login + Control

Owner, Manager, Cashier, Rider — role-based access. Owner sees daily sales, stock, rider performance from phone anywhere.

5. 🛵 Rider App + Live Tracking

Our new 2026 features: Assign order to rider in 1 click, customer sees live status from packing to delivered. No more "rider kahan hai?" calls.

6. 🔍 SEO Ranking Boost

When someone searches "grocery store near me in DHA" or "online mart in Gulshan", your branded website ranks on Google. Free organic customers daily.

7. 💬 WhatsApp Alerts + Repeat Customers

Every order on your WhatsApp instantly. Plus you can send push notifications & WhatsApp offers — "Aaj atta par 5% off" — to bring old customers back. Impossible with offline-only.

8. 🏆 Brand Value Like Imtiaz

Customers trust a mart with its own app more than a WhatsApp list. Your mart looks premium, not a kiryana.
