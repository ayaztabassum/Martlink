Grocery Mobile App Ki Cost Kin Cheezon Par Depend Karti Hai?

Har grocery business ki requirements different hoti hain. App banwane se pehle in important components ko samajhna zaroori hai:

1
Customer Mobile App
Customer products browse kare, search kare, cart mein items add kare aur order place kare.
2
Admin Panel
Products, categories, prices, orders, customers aur basic store operations ko manage karne ke liye central dashboard.
3
Delivery / Rider System
Orders ko riders tak assign karna, delivery status update karna aur delivery workflow manage karna.
4
Website & Ordering Channel
Mobile app ke saath branded website rakhne se customers ko browser se bhi order karne ka option milta hai.

Basic Grocery App vs Complete Grocery System

Sabse pehle decide karein ke aapko sirf customer-facing app chahiye ya complete business system.

  • Basic App: Product listing, categories, cart aur order placement.
  • Standard App: Basic features ke saath customer accounts, order management aur payment/COD options.
  • Complete System: Customer app, website, admin panel, rider workflow, WhatsApp alerts aur business management features.

Grocery Ordering App Mein Kaun Se Features Hone Chahiye?

Professional grocery app banwate waqt sirf attractive design par focus na karein. Customer aur store dono ke liye practical features zyada important hain.

Customer Features

  • Product categories aur search
  • Product details aur pricing
  • Shopping cart
  • Order placement
  • Cash on Delivery aur available online payment options
  • Order status
  • Delivery address management

Admin Features

  • Products add, edit aur remove karna
  • Categories manage karna
  • Prices update karna
  • Orders dekhna aur process karna
  • Customer information manage karna
  • Sales aur order workflow monitor karna

Rider Features

  • Assigned orders dekhna
  • Order pickup aur delivery status update karna
  • Customer delivery information access karna
  • Delivery workflow manage karna

Sirf App Banwana Ya Complete Business System?

Grocery business ke liye sirf mobile app banwa dena aksar enough nahi hota. Agar customer app mein order place kare lekin store ke paas orders manage karne ka proper system na ho, to manual work barh sakta hai.

Is liye better approach ye hai ke customer ordering + admin management + delivery workflow ko ek connected system ke taur par plan kiya jaye.

App Banwane Se Pehle Ye 7 Questions Zaroor Poochhein

  1. App ke saath admin panel included hai?
  2. Website bhi included hai ya separate cost hogi?
  3. Android aur iOS support milega?
  4. Products aur prices update karne ka easy system hai?
  5. Rider/delivery management available hai?
  6. Future mein features add karwana possible hai?
  7. Maintenance aur support ka process kya hai?
