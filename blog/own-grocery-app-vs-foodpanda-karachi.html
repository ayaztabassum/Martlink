Karachi Mart Owners ko Foodpanda / Pandamart Par Depend Kyun Nahi Rehna Chahiye? 7 Bara Nuqsan

By MartLink PK Team (Ayaz Tabassum) | May 15, 2026 | Karachi, Pakistan | 9 min read

Karachi me har dusra super mart owner aaj Foodpanda ya Pandamart par list hai. Start me lagta hai order aa rahe hain, lekin 3 mahine baad hisaab lagao to profit gayab, customer gayab, aur brand bhi khatam.

Agar aap DHA, Gulshan, Johar, North Nazimabad ya PECHS me apna mart chalate hain, to ye blog aapke liye hai. Hum MartLink Solutions me roz 5-6 mart owners se milte hain jo Foodpanda chhod kar apna system banana chahte hain. Kyun? Wajah neeche hai.

⚠️ Haqeeqat Check: Foodpanda / Pandamart aapko customer nahi de raha, aapse customer le raha hai. Aapka customer, unka app, unka data, aur unka profit.

7 Bade Nuqsan Jo Foodpanda / Pandamart Par Rehne Se Hote Hain

1
20-35% Commission - Din Dahare Loot
Aap 1000 Rs ka saman bechte ho, Foodpanda 250-350 Rs kaat leta hai. Aapko milta hai 650-750 Rs. 100 orders roz ke = 25,000-35,000 Rs roz ka nuqsan. Mahine ka 7-10 Lakh! Is se behtar aap apna rider rakh lo 30,000 me.
2
Aapka Customer, Unka Ho Gaya
Foodpanda par customer ko aapka number nahi milta, aapko uska number nahi milta. Aap usko Eid offer, Ramzan deal WhatsApp nahi kar sakte. Agla order wo Pandamart se kar lega kyunki usko sasta dikhega. Aapka loyal customer khatam.
3
Brand Ka Janaza
Foodpanda par aapka mart "Gulshan Mart" 50 aur marts ke beech list me hai. Upar Imtiaz, neeche Chase, beech me aap. Customer ko yaad hi nahi rehta usne kahan se liya. Apni app me "Gulshan Mart - Apni Mart, Apni Pehchan" hota hai. Logo aapka, naam aapka, yaad bhi aapki.
4
Price Control Khatam - Neechy Becho Majboori Me
Foodpanda kehta hai "Discount do warna ranking neeche". Aapko 10% extra discount dena padta hai. Commission upar se, discount upar se. 1000 ki cheez 900 ki bech ke 600 aapko milta hai. Is se behtar dukaan band kar do.
5
Data Ka Kala Dhandha
Aapko pata bhi nahi chalta kaunsa product zyada bik raha, kaunsa area zyada order kar raha, kaunsa customer har hafte 5000 ka leta hai. Ye sara data Foodpanda ke paas hai. Apne system me aapko full analytics milta hai - kaunsa customer chhod gaya, usko wapas kaise lana hai.
6
Payment 7-15 Din Baad - Cash Flow Khatam
Foodpanda payment hafton baad deta hai. Aapko maal udhar lana padta hai. Apne system me JazzCash, Easypaisa, COD sab same day aapke account me. Cash flow chalta rehta hai.
7
Account Block Ka Dar - Ek Review Pe Dukaan Band
Ek fake review, ek rider ki ghalti, aur aapka Foodpanda account suspend. 6 mahine ki mehnat khatam. Apna system hai to koi baap bhi band nahi kar sakta. 100% control aapka.

Final Baat - Aap Dukaan Chalate Ho Ya Foodpanda Ki Naukri?

Foodpanda par aap unke liye kaam kar rahe ho. Apni app par aap apne liye brand bana rahe ho. Imtiaz, Chase, Carrefour kabhi Foodpanda par depend nahi rahe - unki apni app hai. Aap bhi wahi karo.
