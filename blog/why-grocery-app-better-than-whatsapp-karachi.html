WhatsApp Orders vs Grocery App: Karachi Mart Ke Liye Kaunsa Better Hai? (2026)

By MartLink PK Team (Ayaz Tabassum) | May 15, 2026 | Karachi, Pakistan | 8 min read

Karachi ka har mart owner shuruat WhatsApp se karta hai. Customer message karta hai: "Bhai 2kg cheeni, 1 oil, 3 milk bhej do". Start me easy lagta hai, lekin jab roz ke 50+ messages aane lage to WhatsApp ek zeher ban jata hai.

Hum MartLink pe roz aise 10 mart owners se milte hain jo kehte hain: "Bhai WhatsApp par orders manage nahi ho rahe, 5-6 orders roz miss ho jate hain, staff pareshan hai."

Aaj hum seedha comparison karenge — WhatsApp vs Apni Grocery App — taake aap faisla kar sako.

WhatsApp Par Order Lene Ke 5 Bade Nuqsan (Jo Aap Roz Seh Rahe Ho)

1
Order Miss = Customer Hamesha Ke Liye Gaya
Customer ne 11:02 AM pe message kiya, aapka ladka 11:25 pe dekhta hai. Customer ne pehle hi dusri dukaan se manga liya. 1 order miss = 2000 Rs ka nuqsan, plus wo customer ab aapko message hi nahi karega. MartLink App me order ka instant notification, beep sound, aur auto-assign rider — 0 miss.
2
Rate Likhne Me Ghalti = Jeb Se Paise Gaye
WhatsApp par: Cheeni 175 likhna tha, 157 likh diya. Oil 950 ka tha, 850 bol diya. Customer kahega screenshot hai mere paas. Roz ki 3-4 ghaltiyan = 1500-2000 ka loss. App me price ek baar set — customer khud dekhta hai, ghalti ka chance zero.
3
Raatein Kharab - 11 Baje Bhi "Bhai Anda Hai?"
WhatsApp ka koi time nahi. Raat 11:30 pe bhi message. Aap so rahe ho, customer naraz. App me aap timing set karte ho: 9 AM to 11 PM. 11 ke baad app khud kahega "Kal subah delivery hogi" — aapki neend safe.
4
Hisaab Kitaab Ka Azab
Mahine ke end pe pata hi nahi kitne orders hue, kaunsa product zyada bika, kaunsa customer 30,000 ka mahine ka leta hai. WhatsApp chat delete hui to sara data gaya. App me full dashboard: Roz ke orders, best seller, customer lifetime value, rider performance — ek click me.
5
Staff Chori Aur Bahana
"Bhai customer ne 2kg bola tha 1kg nahi" — kaun sach bol raha hai pata hi nahi. App me sab recorded: customer ne jo add to cart kiya wahi rider ko jayega, bill khud banta hai. Chori ka chance khatam.

To App Me Aisa Kya Hai Jo WhatsApp Me Nahi?

✅ App Ka Jadoo - Customer Khud Order Karta Hai:
  • Customer app kholta hai, products dekhta hai (photo ke saath), rate dekhta hai
  • Cart me add karta hai, address select karta hai
  • Payment: COD / JazzCash / Easypaisa — uski marzi
  • Aapko WhatsApp par beep: "Naya Order #1234 - Rs.2450"
  • Rider app par order jata hai, customer ko live tracking milti hai
  • Delivery ke baad customer ko notification: "Rate your order"

Aapne kuch nahi kiya — order khud complete ho gaya!

Real Calculation - WhatsApp Kitna Mehenga Padta Hai?

Faraz Mart, Gulshan - Real Case:
Roz 70 WhatsApp orders, 2 ladke WhatsApp sambhalte hain (25k + 25k = 50k salary).
Roz 4 orders miss (avg 1500) = 6000/day = 1.8 Lakh/month loss
Rate mistakes = 40k/month
Total loss = 50k salary + 1.8 Lakh miss + 40k mistake = 2.7 Lakh/month WhatsApp ki wajah se!

App ke baad: 1 ladka enough, 0 miss, 0 mistake, 40k monthly MartLink fee. Saving = 2.3 Lakh/month!

Kya WhatsApp Band Kar Dena Chahiye? Nahi!

Best strategy jo hum top marts ko dete hain:

Smart Formula: App + WhatsApp Together (MartLink ka System)
  1. Order App se: Customer app se order kare — 90% orders
  2. Notification WhatsApp par: Har naya order aapko WhatsApp par bhi aayega (MartLink ka feature) — aapko app kholne ki zarurat bhi nahi
  3. WhatsApp par sirf support: "Bhai mera order kahan hai?" — iske liye WhatsApp use karo, order lene ke liye nahi

Final Faisla - Kab Tak WhatsApp Par Rahoge?

WhatsApp dukaan ke liye nahi, dosti ke liye hai. 2026 me Karachi me jo mart 100+ orders roz karta hai, wo WhatsApp par nahi tik sakta.

Imtiaz, Chase, Naheed — koi bhi WhatsApp par order nahi leta. Unki apni app hai. Kyunki unhe pata hai brand app se banta hai, WhatsApp se nahi.

Aap bhi abhi chote ho to WhatsApp theek hai. Lekin agar aapko bhi 200+ orders/day karne hain, brand banana hai, to app lena farz hai.
